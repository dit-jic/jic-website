Specialized Unit i

Interventional Cardiology

Provides minimally invasive catheter-based diagnostic and therapeutic procedures for acute and chronic coronary diseases.

Coronary Angiography & PCI

Diagnostic coronary catheterization, primary PCI balloon angioplasty, and complex stenting.

Structural Heart Interventions

TAVI/TAVR procedures, balloon valvuloplasty, and septal defect closures.

Specialized Unit ii

Adult Cardiac Surgery

Delivers advanced surgical interventions for complex coronary artery diseases, valvular heart conditions, and aortic root reconstructions.

CABG (Bypass Surgery)

Off-pump beating heart bypass and total arterial revascularization.

Valve & Aortic Surgery

Mitral and aortic valve repairs/replacements and emergency aortic dissection care.

Specialized Unit iii

Heart Failure Program

Multidisciplinary clinical management, mechanical circulatory support, and evaluation for end-stage cardiac failure and transplant options.

Chronic Management

Specialized medication titration, fluid monitoring, and outpatient heart failure clinics.

Maryam Nawaz Transplant Center

Pre-transplant evaluation, donor management, and post-transplant surveillance.

Specialized Unit iv

Non-Interventional Cardiology

Non-invasive diagnostic procedures to assess cardiac performance, valve function, and electrical rhythm.

Echocardiography

2D/3D Echo, Transesophageal Echo (TEE), and Stress Echo.

ETT (Exercise Stress Test)

Categorized Here

Exercise Stress Testing to detect exercise-induced ischemia. *(Categorized under Non-interventional diagnostics as per guidelines)*.

Rhythm Diagnostics

24/48-Hour Holter monitoring and Ambulatory Blood Pressure profiling.

Specialized Unit v

Imaging Services

High-resolution anatomical and functional radiologic imaging of cardiovascular structures.

CT Angiography

Multi-slice Cardiac CT for non-invasive coronary imaging and calcium scoring.

Cardiac MRI

3T MRI for myocardial viability, cardiomyopathies, and congenital heart assessment.

Gamma Camera

Nuclear SPECT myocardial perfusion imaging for ischemia evaluation.

Specialized Unit vi

Pathology and Diagnostic Services

24/7 automated clinical laboratory services delivering accurate diagnostic testing for acute cardiac markers and systemic pathology.

Cardiac Biomarkers

High-sensitivity Troponin I/T, CK-MB, NT-proBNP, and lipid profiles.

General Pathology

Hematology, blood bank cross-matching, clinical biochemistry, and coagulation monitoring.

Emergency Services

24/7 acute chest pain resuscitation, primary PCI pathway, and emergency care.

Out-patient Services

Daily consultant clinics, specialized follow-ups, and diagnostic screenings.

Preventive Cardiology

Early risk assessment, hypertension/lipid management, and rehab counseling.
