Chief Minister's Message

Leadership Vision for Jinnah Institute of Cardiology

Home / CM's Message

Maryam Nawaz Sharif

Chief Minister Punjab

It is a matter of immense pride and satisfaction to witness the rapid operationalization of the Jinnah Institute of Cardiology (JIC) in Lahore—a landmark healthcare initiative designed to provide world-class, specialized cardiovascular care to the people of Punjab right at their doorstep.

Cardiovascular diseases demand immediate, advanced, and uncompromised medical attention. With the establishment of the state-of-the-art Maryam Nawaz Heart Transplant Unit within the institute, JIC is poised to bridge a critical healthcare gap in our region. This advanced facility will offer hope and advanced life-saving interventions, ensuring that patients requiring complex heart surgeries and heart failure management no longer need to travel abroad for the treatment they deserve.

Our vision is rooted in making quality healthcare accessible, equitable, and patient-centric. The dedication of our medical professionals, administrative leadership, and support staff at JIC embodies our unwavering commitment to public welfare. I extend my heartiest congratulations to the entire team and reaffirm the Government of the Punjab's continuous support in making Punjab healthier, stronger, and more resilient.
