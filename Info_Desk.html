Outpatient Services

OPD Schedule

The Outpatient Department (OPD) at JIC operates on a structured schedule to ensure timely consultation, physical queue management, and streamlined E-prescriptions.

General OPD Timings

Operating Days & Hours: Monday to Saturday: 08:00 AM – 03:00 PM
Registration Desk Hours: 08:00 AM – 03:00 PM
Sunday & Public Holidays: Closed (Emergency services remain fully operational 24/7)
24/7 Critical Response

Emergency Information

Always Active

The JIC Emergency Department operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, delivering rapid intervention for critical cardiac emergencies.

Emergency Services & Features

Immediate Triage

Instant triage classification for patients presenting with severe chest pain, dyspnea, or cardiac arrest.

Dedicated Critical Care Units

On-site Cardiac Care Unit (CCU) and Intensive Care Units equipped with advanced life support and monitoring technology.

Round-the-Clock Diagnostics

Immediate access to emergency ECG, bed-side echocardiography, cardiac biomarker testing, and emergency resuscitation teams.
