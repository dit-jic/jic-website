About JIC Lahore

Committed to Excellence in Cardiac Care

Our Vision

To become a center of international repute in cardiovascular treatment, research, and education, delivering equitable and state-of-the-art care to patients across Punjab and Pakistan.

Our Mission

To provide compassionate, accessible, and high-quality cardiac healthcare while nurturing future healthcare professionals through cutting-edge education, clinical training, and clinical research.

Maryam Nawaz Sharif

Chief Minister Punjab

Message from the Chief Minister Punjab

It gives me immense pleasure and satisfaction to see the Jinnah Institute of Cardiology (JIC), Lahore, become a reality. OUR MEDICAL SERVICES

Comprehensive Cardiac Care

Specialized units spanning from prevention to advanced heart transplants.

Emergency Services

24/7 dedicated acute cardiac emergency care equipped with modern resuscitation protocols.

Out-Patient Department (OPD)

Daily specialist consultations, diagnostic screening, and follow-up clinics.

Interventional Cardiology

Coronary angiography, primary PCI, complex stenting, and structural interventions.

Non-Invasive Cardiology

Echocardiography (2D/3D/TEE), ETT, Holter monitoring, and vascular Doppler.
