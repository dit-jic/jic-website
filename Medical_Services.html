24/7 Critical Response

Emergency Services

Always Open

The Emergency Department provides 24/7 rapid triage, continuous cardiac monitoring, and immediate life-saving interventions for acute cardiovascular events.

Triage & Rapid Assessment

Ensures immediate vital sign stabilization and rapid chest pain risk evaluation upon arrival.

Resuscitation & Acute Care

Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS), invasive hemodynamic monitoring, emergency intubation, and continuous cardiac telemetry.

Primary PCI Capability

Direct access pathway to catheterization laboratories for immediate balloon angioplasty and stenting in acute myocardial infarction.

Acute Stroke & Aortic Protocol

Fast-track multi-slice CT angiography for acute aortic dissection, pulmonary embolism, and cerebrovascular events.

Consultation & Care

Out-Patient Department (OPD)

Provides consultation, evaluation, diagnosis, and follow-up care for patients with cardiovascular conditions.

Specialist Consultations

Diagnostic evaluations, risk stratification, and specialized clinical assessments for routine and complex cardiovascular conditions.

Specialized Clinics

Dedicated clinics for Heart Failure, Hypertension, Arrhythmia & Pacemaker, Valve Disease, and Post-Surgical Follow-up.

Diagnostic Integration

Direct coordination with non-invasive diagnostics for same-day ECG, Echocardiography, ETT, and laboratory investigations.

Long-Term Management

Comprehensive chronic disease monitoring, medication titration, lifestyle counseling, and secondary prevention planning.

Medical Management

Cardiology Services

Offers comprehensive medical evaluation and management of a wide range of cardiovascular diseases.

Comprehensive Clinical Care

Diagnosis, risk assessment, and management of coronary artery disease, heart failure, valvular heart disease, and hypertension.

Non-Invasive Diagnostics

Advanced diagnostic integration including 12-lead ECG, 2D/3D Echocardiography, Exercise Stress Testing (ETT), Holter monitoring, and Ambulatory BP tracking.

Specialized Clinics

Dedicated disease-management clinics for Heart Failure, Lipid Management, Cardiac Rehabilitation, and Post-Procedure follow-ups.

Interventional Integration

Direct linkage with catheterization laboratories for diagnostic angiography, angioplasty (PCI), and structural heart care.

Catheter-Based Procedures

Interventional Cardiology

Provides minimally invasive catheter-based procedures for the diagnosis and treatment of cardiovascular conditions.

Coronary Interventions

Diagnostic Coronary Angiography, Primary and Elective Percutaneous Coronary Intervention (PCI / Angioplasty), and Stenting.

Complex Coronary Care

Chronic Total Occlusion (CTO) interventions, Rotational Atherectomy, Intravascular Ultrasound (IVUS), and Fractional Flow Reserve (FFR) assessment.

Structural Heart Disease

Transcatheter Aortic Valve Implantation (TAVI/TAVR), Balloon Valvuloplasty, and Atrial/Ventricular Septal Defect closures.

Device & Vascular Interventions

Permanent Pacemaker Implantation, Implantable Cardioverter Defibrillator (ICD) placement, Cardiac Resynchronization Therapy (CRT), and Peripheral Vascular Angioplasty.

Diagnostic Investigations

Non-Invasive Cardiology

Provides advanced non-invasive cardiac investigations to assess heart structure, function, and overall cardiovascular health.

Echocardiography Services

Comprehensive 2D/3D, TEE, and stress echocardiograms to evaluate heart valves, chambers, and pumping strength.

Stress & Rhythm Diagnostics

Exercise Tolerance Testing (ETT) and Holter rhythm tracking to detect ischemia and arrhythmias.

Vascular Diagnostics

Ambulatory blood pressure profiling and Doppler ultrasound assessment of major peripheral vessels.

Advanced Diagnostic Imaging

CT Coronary Angiography and nuclear myocardial perfusion studies for accurate non-surgical diagnosis.

Rhythm Disorders

Electrophysiology

Specializes in the diagnosis and management of heart rhythm disorders through advanced electrophysiological evaluation and treatment.

Advanced Arrhythmia Evaluation

High-precision diagnostic electrophysiology studies (EPS) to pinpoint electrical system abnormalities of the heart.

Catheter Ablation

State-of-the-art radiofrequency and cryoablation therapies to restore normal heart rhythms in AFib, SVT, and VT.

Cardiac Rhythm Devices

Implantation and management of pacemakers, defibrillators (ICD), and biventricular devices (CRT) for heart failure.

Comprehensive Rhythm Care

Specialized clinics for syncope evaluation, inherited arrhythmia syndromes, and device follow-up optimization.

Early Risk Management

Preventive Cardiology

Focuses on early risk assessment, prevention, and management of cardiovascular disease through lifestyle and clinical interventions.

Cardiovascular Risk Assessment

Early detection through lipid profiling, advanced lipid biomarkers, high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hs-CRP), and coronary calcium scoring.

Metabolic & Hypertension Control

Comprehensive management of resistant hypertension, dyslipidemia, metabolic syndrome, and cardiovascular risk in diabetic patients.

Lifestyle & Behavioral Medicine

Tailored nutritional counseling, structured smoking cessation programs, weight management plans, and stress reduction strategies.

Family & Genetic Risk Evaluation

Screening and early intervention for familial hypercholesterolemia, premature coronary artery disease, and inherited cardiovascular conditions.

Surgical Specialties

Adult Cardiac Surgery

Provides specialized surgical treatment for adults with complex cardiac conditions.

Coronary Artery Bypass Surgery (CABG)

Advanced surgical options including off-pump (beating heart) and total arterial revascularization.

Heart Valve Repair & Replacement

Comprehensive surgical restoration for diseased mitral, aortic, and tricuspid valves.

Aortic Surgery

Emergency and elective surgical management of complex aortic aneurysms and acute aortic dissections.

Complex Adult Interventions

Specialized treatment for cardiac tumors, structural heart defects, and high-risk surgical revisions.

Advanced Heart Failure & Organ Replacement

Maryam Nawaz Heart Transplant Center

Provides specialized multidisciplinary care for patients with advanced heart failure, including evaluation and heart transplantation services.

Advanced Heart Failure Care

Expert clinical management and decision-making for end-stage cardiovascular disease.

Comprehensive Transplant Services

Full pre-transplant candidate evaluation, donor coordination, and surgical transplantation services.

Mechanical Assist Devices

Implantation and maintenance of Left Ventricular Assist Devices (LVADs) as a bridge-to-transplant or destination therapy.

Dedicated Post-Op Surveillance

Specialized ICU monitoring, routine rejection screening, and tailored long-term immunosuppression regimens.

Next-Gen Surgical Techniques

Minimally Invasive Cardiac Surgery

Provides advanced surgical techniques designed to treat cardiac conditions through smaller incisions, supporting faster recovery and reduced surgical impact.

Smaller Incisions & Minimal Trauma

Avoids full sternotomy (chest-splitting) by operating through small mini-thoracotomy access ports.

Advanced Valve & Bypass Procedures

Enables precise mitral/aortic valve interventions and single/multi-vessel coronary bypass.

Faster Recovery & Rehabilitation

Reduces hospital stay, minimizes post-operative pain, lowers blood transfusion requirements, and accelerates return to daily activities.

Enhanced Cosmetic Outcomes

Substantially smaller surgical scars compared to traditional open-heart procedures.
