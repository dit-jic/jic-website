Chief Minister's Message

Leadership Vision for Jinnah Institute of Cardiology

Home / CM's Message

Maryam Nawaz Sharif

Chief Minister Punjab

It gives me immense pleasure and satisfaction to see the Jinnah Institute of Cardiology (JIC), Lahore, become a reality. This institute has been one of my cherished dreams, and its inauguration marks an important milestone in our commitment to providing modern, advanced and free cardiac healthcare to the people.

Heart disease has become an increasingly serious public health challenge, affecting thousands of families across our country. The establishment of JIC, equipped with state-of-the-art cardiac facilities, advanced medical technology, modern catheterization laboratories, internationally equipped wards and highly trained medical professionals, represents a significant step towards addressing this challenge. The establishment of the Maryam Nawaz Heart Transplant Centre is particularly significant, as it brings Pakistan's first-of-its-kind cardiac transplant facility within the reach of patients who previously faced enormous financial and geographical barriers to accessing such treatment. Through a dedicated heart transplant fund and the use of advanced technology, we are determined to make even the most complex and expensive cardiac procedures accessible to deserving patients, completely free of cost.

Our commitment to cardiac healthcare extends well beyond this institution. The successful treatment of thousands of children suffering from congenital heart diseases, the expansion of catheterization laboratories across Punjab and our initiatives in stroke treatment reflect our determination to ensure that quality healthcare is no longer confined to major cities. We are equally committed to expanding initiatives such as the Punjab Air Ambulance service, home dialysis and Clinics on Wheels to bring essential healthcare services closer to the people.

I firmly believe that public office is a responsibility and an opportunity to serve humanity. Every decision, every initiative and every public institution must ultimately translate into relief and betterment in the lives of ordinary citizens. While we have made significant progress, our resolve remains to continuously improve healthcare delivery, strengthen accountability and ensure that every patient receives treatment with dignity, compassion and care.

I congratulate the doctors, healthcare professionals, administrative leadership, engineers and everyone whose dedication and hard work have made the Jinnah Institute of Cardiology possible. The Government of Punjab will continue to extend its full support to this institution and its mission. Our vision is clear: no deserving patient should be deprived of quality treatment because of financial constraints or lack of access to advanced medical facilities.

“Our commitment is to save lives, serve people and build a healthier Punjab”.
