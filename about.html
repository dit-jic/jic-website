Institutional Profile

Overview

Vision

To be Pakistan's and the region's leading centre of excellence in cardiac care, where innovation in heart transplantation and advanced cardiology saves lives, advances medical knowledge and inspires hope, ensuring that no patient in Pakistan has to travel abroad for the heart care they deserve.

Mission

To establish Jinnah Institute of Cardiology as a center of excellence in cardiovascular care, including advanced cardiac interventions and transplantation, with a commitment to patient safety, clinical excellence and innovation.

Background & Establishment

Jinnah Institute of Cardiology (JIC), Lahore was established to strengthen specialized cardiac healthcare services and address the growing need for advanced cardiovascular treatment in Lahore and surrounding districts.

The institute was developed as a modern, comprehensive cardiac care facility equipped with advanced diagnostic, interventional, surgical, emergency, and critical care services. Its establishment reflects a commitment to expanding access to quality cardiac healthcare, particularly for patients requiring timely and specialized treatment.

Jinnah Institute of Cardiology aims to serve as a center of excellence in cardiovascular care, combining modern medical technology, skilled healthcare professionals, patient-centered services, medical education, and continuous improvement in cardiac treatment and management.

Institutional Hierarchy

Organogram

The organizational structure of Jinnah Institute of Cardiology (JIC), Lahore is designed to ensure effective leadership, efficient administration, coordinated clinical services, and quality patient care.

The organogram defines the roles, responsibilities, reporting lines, and coordination among clinical, administrative, nursing, technical, and support departments.

This structured hierarchy promotes teamwork, accountability, transparency, and efficient decision-making across the institute, supporting the delivery of comprehensive and patient-centered cardiac healthcare services.

Jinnah Institute of Cardiology (JIC) is a premier public sector cardiac institute in Lahore dedicated to specialized patient care, research, and medical education.
