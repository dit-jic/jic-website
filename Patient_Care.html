Inpatient Care Management

Admission & Discharge Information

Admission Policy

  • All cardiac admissions will be done on doctor's advice after initial assessment in Emergency / OPD.
  • Patient file, ECG, ECHO and relevant investigations must be completed and documented at time of admission.
  • High-risk / emergency cardiac cases (Chest Pain, MI, Arrhythmia) will be admitted on priority basis and informed to on-call consultant immediately.

Discharge Policy

  • Discharge will be made only after consultant's written order and stable vitals.
  • Final diagnosis, discharge summary, prescribed cardiac medicines, diet, and lifestyle instructions must be clearly explained to patient / family.
  • Follow-up date, cardiac rehab, and warning signs (chest pain, dyspnea, swelling) must be explained before discharge, with complete file and documentation.
Ethical Care Standards

Patient Rights & Responsibilities

Patient Rights

Every cardiac patient has the right to receive respectful, timely, and safe care, to be informed about their heart condition and treatment plan, and to give consent after understanding risks and benefits.

Patient Responsibilities

The patient is responsible to provide complete medical history, follow prescribed medicines, diet and lifestyle advice, and to inform staff immediately about any chest pain, breathing difficulty or change in condition.
