Patient Rights & Responsibilities

Patient Rights

Every cardiac patient has the right to receive respectful, timely, and safe care, to be informed about their heart condition and treatment plan, and to give consent after understanding risks and benefits.

Patient Responsibilities

The patient is responsible to provide complete medical history, follow prescribed medicines, diet and lifestyle advice, and to inform staff immediately about any chest pain, breathing difficulty or change in condition.

Global Healthcare Assistance

International Patients

Jinnah Institute of Cardiology (JIC) offers dedicated, high-quality cardiovascular care to international patients seeking specialized heart treatment in Pakistan. From pre-arrival evaluation to post-discharge care, comprehensive support is provided to ensure a seamless medical journey.

Pre-Arrival Evaluation

Remote record review and pre-procedure consultation prior to travel.

Dedicated Coordination

Fast-track admissions, translation support, and logistics assistance.

Post-Discharge Follow-Up

Tele-consultations and continuity of care after returning home.

Operational Excellence

Support Services

JIC Support Services provide the essential operational foundation that ensures safe, efficient, and compassionate patient care across all clinical departments.

Emergency & OPD Triage

Initial patient assessment and priority management for acute cardiac emergencies.

Cardiac Diagnostics

24/7 diagnostic support including ECG, Echocardiography (ECHO), angiography, and cardiac pathology services.

Pharmacy & Therapeutics

Provision of cardiac medications alongside clear administration and safety guidance.
